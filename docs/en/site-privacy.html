Site privacy and cookies

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. We do not ask for consent because there is nothing to consent to.

Effective from November 15, 2026

Cookies

None. The site sets no cookies, neither ours nor third-party.

What stays in your browser

Only two preferences you choose, saved in the browser's local storage and never sent to anyone:

NameValueWhen
lemora-temalight or darkIf you use the light/dark switch
lemora-linguait or enIf you switch language with the IT/EN button

They are strictly technical: they only remember your choice. Delete them any time from your browser settings (site data).

Analytics and third parties

No analytics, advertising or tracking. Fonts, images and scripts are all hosted here: opening the site does not make your browser contact Google Fonts, CDNs or other services.

Who hosts the site

The site is published with GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub may log technical request data such as your IP address for security and to run the service. GitHub processes it under its privacy statement; it does not reach us.

Links to other sites

Links to GitHub (releases, issues) and to the sites we mention take you elsewhere: from then on, those sites' rules apply.

Browser extension

The “Lemora Web Extension” extension has its own policy: extension privacy.

Contact

For questions: the project's issues on GitHub. If this page changes, the date at the top is updated.
