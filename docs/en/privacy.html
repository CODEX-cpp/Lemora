Extension “Lemora: password dei dispositivi” (device passwords) · Chrome, Edge, Firefox · version 1.0.0

Browser extension privacy policy

In short: the extension has no account, stores no passwords and sends no data off your PC. It only talks to the Lemora app installed on the same PC.

Effective from November 15, 2026

1. Purpose

The single purpose of the extension is to fill, in web pages, the device passwords (routers, printers, NAS, firewalls, management panels) saved in the Lemora desktop app for Windows, and to save new or changed logins into Lemora. Without Lemora installed the extension does nothing.

2. Data handled

DataWhenWhy
Address of the open page (scheme, site name, port)When you open the popup or a page with a login formTo find the logins of that device in Lemora
User name and password of a Lemora entryOnly when you click “Fill” or “Copy”To fill the form or copy them to the clipboard
Entries you save or edit (title, address, user, password, folder)Only when you confirm savingTo write them into Lemora's encrypted database
Pairing key with Lemora and recently used entries (id and time)After pairingTo be recognised by Lemora and show recent entries

The extension does not record the pages you visit, does not read other fields of the page and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking.

3. Where the data goes

Everything stays on your PC. The extension only communicates with the Lemora app of the same Windows user, through the browser's local channel (native messaging, host com.lemora.passwords). Lemora answers only the extension you paired and confirmed. Passwords live in Lemora's database, encrypted with AES-GCM and unlocked with your master password or Windows Hello.

No data is sent to the developer or to third parties. If you use Lemora in Teams mode, the Lemora app (not the extension) syncs entries with your organisation's Teams server and logs their use for administrators, under your organisation's rules.

4. Permissions

nativeMessaging
To talk to the Lemora app on your PC. It is the only channel the extension uses.
storage
Only to move the pairing key of earlier versions into the extension's internal storage.
tabs
To read the address of the active tab, update the counter on the icon and send the fill to the right tab.
Site access (http and https)
Devices have arbitrary addresses, often local IPs: the extension must be able to show the Lemora icon in the login fields of any page. It fills only when you ask.

The extension does not download or run remote code.

5. Retention and deletion

Passwords stay in Lemora's encrypted database, which you control. To unpair the extension: in Lemora, Settings › Credentials › Browser extension › Unpair. Uninstalling the extension makes the browser delete its internal storage too (pairing key and recent entries).

6. Limited Use

The use of information received through the extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • data is used only for the single purpose described above;
  • it is not transferred to anyone, except where you need it (for example to your organisation's Teams server, by the Lemora app) or where the law requires it;
  • it is not used for advertising, not sold and not used to determine creditworthiness;
  • no human reads your data.

7. Contact and changes

For questions or reports: the project's issue page on GitHub. If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated and the changes are described here.
